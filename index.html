Charger math

The phone goes from 20% to 80% while you make coffee, the old brick in the drawer says 5 W, and the new one promises the world in watts. Charger math turns capacity and watts into honest minutes, a charging habit into its yearly kWh, and an upgrade into the time it actually buys back. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Charge plan - capacity, watts and a percent range become the minutes on the cable.
  • Year plan - a charging habit becomes kWh a year and what that costs.
  • Upgrade plan - old charger versus new becomes minutes saved per charge and hours a year.

Labeled norms

Labeled cell voltage: 3.7 V nominal. Labeled charge efficiency: 80% - some of the wall power leaves as heat before it reaches the cell. Labeled reference tariff: 0.15 per kWh; put in your own. These are reference values, not rules - real charging slows near full and varies by device, so treat the minutes as honest estimates.
